Ink
Brawl

Two stick figures, one notebook page. Drain the other one's health to win the round. Best of three.

Esc pauses. M mutes sound.

This game needs a keyboard.

Controls and combos

Moving

A DWalk
WJump, press again for a double jump
SHold to guard. In the air, fall faster. Tap twice on a thin ledge to drop through.
SpaceDodge. Hold a direction to dash.

On the ground

JJab. JJJ ends in a spin kick.
JJKJab string into a heavy punch
KHeavy punch
A/D+KRocket punch, dashes forward
W+KUppercut, launches
LKick
A/D+LSlide kick. If it hits, press W to jump after them.
S+LSweep

In the air

JAir jab
KAxe punch, knocks down
LAir kick
W+LFlip kick
S+LStomp dive
W+KUppercut, once per jump. Good for getting back.

Ink specials

IInk shot, costs 20 ink
S D IBig blast, costs 40. Tap down, then a direction, then I.
S+IShockwave on both sides, costs 35
W+IRising dragon, costs 60. Can't be hit while rising.

Ink fills as you land and take hits. Falling off the page costs 15 health. The less health you have, the farther you fly.

Player two

Arrow keys to move, jump and guard. Num 4 5 6 8 work like J K L I (or , . / ; on laptops). Dodge with Num 0 or Right Shift.
