How to play

The idea

There is no health bar. Every hit adds to your damage, and the higher that number climbs the further the next hit sends you. You lose a life by leaving the map, not by running out of health. Three lives each — last one standing wins.

Moving

A D or ← →Run
SpaceJump. Press again in the air for a second jump. Let go early for a short hop.
S or ↓Drop faster. Tap twice on a thin ledge to fall through it.
L or ShiftDodge. You cannot be hit for a moment. Hold a direction to roll that way.

Weapons

Walk over onePicks it up. You can only carry one.
H or EThrow it at someone. It hurts, and then you are empty-handed.

Sword even and quick. Hammer slow but sends people flying. Spear long reach. Blasters shoot from range. Without a weapon your signatures are feeble, so go and find one. Drop it when you are knocked out.

Attacking

Two buttons. J or left click is a quick attack, K or right click is a slow signature. What you get depends on the direction you hold.

JQuick attack. Press it again to keep the string going.
side + JSide attack, good reach
↓ + JLow attack, pops them up
KSignature. Slow, heavy, needs a weapon.
side + KLunging signature
↓ + KGround slam, hits both sides

In the air

JAir attack
side + JSide air
↓ + JDown air. Spikes them toward the floor.
KRecovery. Launches you upward, once per jump. This is how you get back.
↓ + KGround pound. Drops like a stone.

Two on one keyboard

Player one uses W A S D with F attack, G signature, V dodge, B throw. Player two uses the arrow keys with . attack, / signature, Right Shift dodge, ' throw.
