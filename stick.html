StickXenoverse

Two stick warriors. One shattered arena above the cloud sea. Burn the other one's health to zero. Best of three.

Esc pauses  ·  M mutes sound

This game needs a keyboard.

Controls and combos

Moving

A DRun
WJump, press again for a double jump. Let go early for a short hop.
SHold to raise your ki barrier. In the air, dive faster. Tap twice on a thin ledge to drop through.
SpaceStep dodge. Hold a direction to boost. Cancels your own recovery.

On the ground

JJab. JJJ ends in a spin kick.
JJKJab string into a heavy punch
KHeavy punch
A/D+KRush punch, boosts forward
W+KUppercut, launches
LKick
A/D+LSlide kick. If it hits, press W to chase them up.
S+LSweep

In the air

JAir jab
KHammer fist, spikes them down
LAir kick
W+LFlip kick
S+LMeteor dive
W+KUppercut, once per jump. Good for getting back.

Ki attacks

IKi blast, costs 20 ki
S D IWave cannon, costs 40. Tap down, then a direction, then I.
S+IExplosive wave on both sides, costs 35
W+IRising dragon, costs 60. Super armour on the way up.

Ki charges as you land hits and take them. At 60 ki your aura ignites and your hair burns gold. Falling out of the arena costs 15 health. The less health you have, the farther you fly.

Player two

Arrow keys to move, jump and guard. Num 4 5 6 8 work like J K L I (or , . / ; on laptops). Dodge with Num 0 or Right Shift.
