SKYBREAK

Aerial brawl · netcode lab

Skybreak is played with a keyboard or a gamepad. On a touch-only device you can watch, but not play.

Your inputs are simulated on the frame you press them; the server confirms later and corrections are smoothed. Press F3 in a match to watch it happen.

Match setup

Host an online match

Your tab is the authoritative server. Invite each friend with a code, paste back their reply, then start. You can move around while you wait.

    No signalling server is used: codes are exchanged by hand (chat, email). Connections use public STUN only, with no TURN relay, so two players on strict/symmetric NATs may be unable to connect.
